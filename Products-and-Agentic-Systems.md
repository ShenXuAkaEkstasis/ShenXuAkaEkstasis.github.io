## DeepSeek Harness Plugins

### DSH AI Shopping Assistant

A **DeepSeek Harness (DSH)** shopping-decision plugin designed for product comparison and selection.

Its workflow includes candidate ranking, realizable-price analysis, merchant and review evidence, and source-quality risk checks.

GitHub:  
https://github.com/ShenXuAkaEkstasis/dsh-ai-shopping-assistant

The plugin is also indexed in the public DSH community plugin registry:

https://github.com/dshworks/awesome-dsh-plugins/blob/main/lists/usage-cost.md

### DSH AI SaaS Deal Finder

A **DeepSeek Harness (DSH)** plugin that identifies the cheapest legitimate AI/SaaS purchase option that actually applies to a user.

It evaluates factors including region, eligibility, payment methods, taxes, invoicing, renewal terms, and service-policy constraints before ranking usable purchase options.

GitHub:  
https://github.com/ShenXuAkaEkstasis/dsh-ai-saas-deal-finder
