# Shen Xu 徐慎

**Independent AI Visibility Strategist & Researcher | Shanghai, China**

I am Shen Xu (徐慎), an independent AI visibility strategist and researcher based in Shanghai, China, where I have lived and worked for more than a decade.

My work focuses on how AI systems discover, evaluate, compare, select, and increasingly transact with businesses, products, and other entities.

My main research areas are **AI Visibility, Agentic Search Optimization (ASO), Decision Authority Economy, Entity Ranking, and Agent-to-Agent Commerce (A2A Commerce).**

## Research Focus

### AI Visibility

I study how brands, companies, and other entities are represented, retrieved, and recommended by AI systems, and how organizations can improve their visibility in AI-mediated information environments.

### Agentic Search Optimization

My work on **Agentic Search Optimization (ASO)** examines the shift from traditional search ranking toward AI systems that retrieve, evaluate, compare, and select entities on behalf of users.

### Decision Authority Economy

**Decision Authority Economy** is the central framework connecting much of my current research.

It looks at what happens when people delegate parts of search, comparison, recommendation, selection, and execution to AI systems, and how this redistribution of decision authority changes markets and competition.

### Agent-to-Agent Commerce

My recent research extends this framework into **Agent-to-Agent Commerce (A2A Commerce)**.

I am particularly interested in how buyer agents, merchant agents, payment infrastructure, machine-readable product data, plugins, skills, and transaction protocols may create a new decision and execution layer within existing B2B and B2C markets.

I currently describe this progression as:

**Visibility → Eligibility → Selection → Transaction**

### Entity Ranking

I also research how AI systems evaluate the importance and authority of entities.

My 2026 arXiv paper, *Representing Entity Importance in AI Knowledge Systems: A Dual-Signal Framework of Audience Evaluation and Structural Authority*, examines entity importance through audience evaluation and structural authority signals.
